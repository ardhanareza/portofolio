( 02 ) The data chain

Three links
must hold.

For data to flow smoothly from the unit's sensors to the database, every link in the chain has to be in good condition: the wiring, the UMPC, and the CPE.

01Unit sensorsEngine, payload, GPS and status signals
02 · checkWiringCarries sensor signals to the UMPC
03 · checkUMPCRuns FMS and processes the data
04 · checkCPESends processed data out of the unit
05NetworkInternet connection on site
06DatabaseWhere operational data is stored
A.Wiring in good conditionSo the data from the unit's sensors is received by the UMPC properly.
B.UMPC in good conditionSo it can process the volume of data coming in from the sensors.
C.CPE in good conditionSo the data processed by FMS on the UMPC is sent to the database as it should be.

( 05 ) Repair lead time

From 6 hours
to 1.5.

Before the system, a repair took an average of 6 hours: from the breakdown, reporting to dispatch, writing a work order, the mechanic driving out and taking up the unit, until FMS was ready for use again. With auto work orders in one portal and clear mechanic evaluation, report to RFU now averages 1.5 hours. Most of that is travel across the site. The information chain and take-up together take about 15 minutes.
